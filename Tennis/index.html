Basic Rules of Tennis

  • Match is played between two players (singles) or two teams (doubles).
  • Player must serve diagonally into opponent's service box.
  • Points: 15, 30, 40, Game.
  • Player must win by 2 points.
  • Ball must stay within court boundaries.
